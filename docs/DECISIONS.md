# Online Learning Platform --- Decision Log

**Status:** Approved for Phase 1–5 Implementation  
**Version:** 0.2  
**Last updated:** 10 November 2026

This log separates confirmed requirements from recommendations and unresolved choices. Following the Product Owner approval session on 10 November 2026, foundational architecture, database, financial, and provider decisions have been locked.

## 1. Confirmed requirements from product discussion & owner approval

| ID    | Area                          | Confirmed requirement                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Status    | Approved Date |
| :---- | :---------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------- | :------------ |
| C-001 | Product                       | Build an online learning platform.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-002 | Roles                         | Four primary roles/panels: Admin, Student, Teacher, Support Team.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-003 | Login                         | Four separate login pages (`/admin/login`, `/student/login`, `/teacher/login`, `/support/login`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-004 | Live learning                 | Live class functionality is required.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-005 | Referrals                     | Students can refer other students and earn wallet credit under a defined reward policy.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-006 | Wallet                        | Wallet can be used to buy courses and users can request UPI redemption.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-007 | Academics                     | Courses, enrollments, classes, materials, assignments, exams, results, and certificates are required.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-008 | Attendance                    | Teacher generates a daily/course/session-based attendance code; students submit it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-009 | Payments                      | Course-based payments and installment plans are required.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-010 | Notifications                 | Notifications are required.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-011 | Analytics                     | Stats and performance views are required across all four role dashboards.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-012 | Scheduling                    | Schedule/calendar functionality is required.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-013 | Role scope                    | Student sees own performance; teacher sees own performance and assigned-course students; admin sees all; support sees ticket-scoped context.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-014 | Payment scope                 | Student sees own payment history/status; teacher sees own payout/payment history; admin monitors all.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-015 | Backend                       | Separate `backend/` folder, Node.js, Express.js, TypeScript, Prisma, MySQL.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-016 | Backend pattern               | Centralized `ApiError`, `apiResponse`, and `catchAsync`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-017 | Backend modules               | Module-based structure with router, controller, service, validation, and types.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-018 | Frontend                      | Separate `frontend/` folder with Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and modular feature-based design.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-019 | Frontend data                 | TanStack Query is required for server state management.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-020 | Frontend API                  | Centralized Axios API calling utility with auth/refresh interceptors is required.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-021 | Feature structure             | Feature folders contain `types/`, `services/`, `hooks/`, `components/`, `<feature>.constant.ts`, and `index.tsx`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-022 | Recordings                    | Recorded classes hosted externally on YouTube (storing validated video ID/metadata, not raw iframe HTML); all other services and local storage hosted on Hostinger VPS.                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-023 | UI Theme & UX                 | Rich role-specific dashboards matching approved mockups (Admin, Student, Teacher, Support) with full Dark and Light mode support.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-024 | Architecture & Tenancy        | Single-institute ownership model (`O-001`), one modular monolith backend (`P-001`), and one Next.js frontend housing all four role panels (`O-002`, `P-003`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-025 | Primary Key & Auth Strategy   | UUID (`@default(uuid())`) string primary keys across entities; Argon2/bcrypt password hashing; short-lived JWT access token + HttpOnly cookie-based rotating refresh token stored as a SHA-256 hash in `sessions`; single primary role per user account with granular RBAC permissions (`O-004`); open student self-signup and admin-provisioned staff/teacher accounts (`O-005`). | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-026 | Teacher Assignment            | Courses support multiple assigned teachers (`PRIMARY`, `CO_INSTRUCTOR`, `TEACHING_ASSISTANT`) via `course_teacher_assignments` (`O-007`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-027 | Installment Access Policy     | Students selecting an installment plan receive full course access immediately upon verified payment of the 1st installment, with automatic access suspension if a subsequent installment becomes overdue past its grace period (`O-010`, `O-011`).                                                                                                                                 | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-028 | Referral & Wallet Reservation | Referral rewards credit the referrer's wallet upon the referred student's first verified paid enrollment (`O-012`). Submitting a UPI redemption request immediately reserves/deducts funds from the available wallet balance via an append-only ledger entry, and restores balance via a reversal entry if rejected by Admin (`O-014`, `P-007`).                                   | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-029 | Teacher Compensation          | Teacher payouts (`O-015`) use a configurable revenue-share percentage per course assignment, settled in Admin-approved payout batches.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-030 | Infrastructure & Adapters     | Hostinger VPS hosts the Next.js frontend, Express API, MySQL database, and local/S3-compatible file storage; recorded classes use YouTube video IDs (`O-018`, `O-019`, `O-025`). Razorpay (`O-008`, `P-005`) and 100ms (`O-016`, `P-004`) are locked behind provider adapters (`P-006`).                                                                                           | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |
| C-031 | Gamification & AI Assistant   | Student XP/Level-up bars and Live AI Study Assistant widgets are deferred to a later post-MVP phase so Phase 1–5 remains focused on core academic and financial integrity.                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Confirmed | 10 Nov 2026   |

## 2. Secondary operational items (Non-blocking for Phase 1; configurable via platform settings)

| ID    | Area              | Current Default / Status                                                                                                                                                            |
| :---- | :---------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| O-003 | Mobile strategy   | Responsive web first; native mobile app deferred to post-MVP.                                                                                                                       |
| O-009 | Currency          | Default currency `INR` (`₹`) with `DECIMAL(12, 2)` precision across all financial tables.                                                                                           |
| O-021 | Attendance policy | Session-based 6-digit attendance code hashed at rest, 10-minute default expiry, rate-limited (max 5 attempts/min), manual teacher override requires mandatory reason and audit log. |
| O-022 | Exams             | Objective (MCQ/Multi-select/True-False) auto-graded + Subjective manual-graded; correct answers hidden until `resultPublishAt`.                                                     |
| O-023 | Certificates      | Configurable per course (default: >= 75% attendance and >= 50% exam pass threshold); unique verifiable certificate number.                                                          |
